Hybrid Smart Street Light Fault Monitoring & Dispatch

Eliminating the multi-million dollar cost of per-pole cellular sensors by pairing 1 Feeder Monitoring Box per zone for electrical blackouts with Citizen QR reporting for individual bulb burnouts and physical hazards.

System Principles
  • • Group Detection: Identifies feeder trips, zero-voltage, surges across entire circuit lines.
  • • No False Claims: Feeder box never falsely claims to identify an isolated failed bulb.
  • • Instant Geo-Dispatch: QR reports pre-fill exact pole GPS coordinates & landmark for workers.
  • • Deduplication Engine: Active duplicate reports on the same pole are automatically linked.
