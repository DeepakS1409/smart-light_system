Architectural Concept & Feeder Rule:

In this hybrid system, one monitoring box is installed at the group substation feeder for each entire zone (e.g. Zone A). It continuously measures aggregate grid supply (Voltage & Current).

Core Rule: Feeder-level telemetry identifies group-wide electrical outages or abnormal electrical supply. It NEVER isolates or claims to identify a specific burnt-out bulb. Individual bulbs are crowd-reported via citizen QR codes.
