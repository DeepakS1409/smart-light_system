How to Test QR Scanning with Your Smartphone

When running the Flask dev server locally on your laptop, scanning a QR containing 127.0.0.1 or localhost from your phone will not work because the phone looks for the server on itself.

Step 1: Same Wi-Fi Network

Connect both your laptop and your smartphone to the same Wi-Fi network.

Step 2: Find Laptop LAN IP

Open terminal and run ipconfig (Windows). Find your IPv4 address (e.g. 192.168.1.45).

Step 3: Update .env & Run on 0.0.0.0

Set APP_BASE_URL=http://192.168.1.45:5000 and HOST=0.0.0.0 in .env.
